# Knowledge Curator

Periodically scans your docs space, finds stale articles (no edits in 90 days), and proposes link suggestions across topics. Outputs a tidy weekly digest.

## Tutorials

- Find stale articles in our Confluence in the Engineering space
- Suggest 10 cross-links between Product and Eng docs
