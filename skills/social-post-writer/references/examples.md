## 46.2. Instagram

### IG-FINAL-01 — Hiut Denim: detailed product introduction
- Evidence: USER-SUPPLIED
- Pattern: Product identity → fit differentiation → design details → wearing outcome → availability.
- Best for: Product launch where fit/construction is the story.

### IG-FINAL-02 — Kylie Cosmetics: ultra-concise benefit caption
- Evidence: USER-SUPPLIED
- Pattern: Emoji cue → product role → 2–3 benefits → availability.
- Best for: Creative already shows texture/product use.

### IG-FINAL-03 — Cocoon: education-led product story
- Evidence: USER-SUPPLIED
- Pattern: Category problem → technical challenge → formulation approach → user experience → brand philosophy.
- Best for: Complex innovation needing explanation.
- Risk: Technical and clinical details must be source-backed.

### IG-FINAL-04 — Cocoon: launch/specification caption
- Evidence: USER-SUPPLIED
- Pattern: Launch hook → price/date → core promise → benefit groups → test/proof → hashtags.
- Best for: Launch post where caption must carry full product information.

### IG-FINAL-05 — Minimal lifestyle launch pattern
- Evidence: GOLD-SYNTHETIC, derived from observed patterns of product-first DTC brands.
- Pattern: One sensory sentence → one practical detail → availability.

#### GOLD-IG-05

Mát, gọn và đủ nhẹ cho những ngày di chuyển liên tục.

Daily Flex kết hợp cotton compact với form regular để giữ cảm giác thoải mái mà vẫn chỉn chu khi mặc cả ngày.
