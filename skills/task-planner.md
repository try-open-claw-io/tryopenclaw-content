# Task Planner

Reads a goal, decomposes it into a tree of tasks, assigns realistic durations, and emits a Gantt-friendly plan. Optional dependency graph and risk register.

## Tutorials

- Plan the launch of our new pricing page over 4 weeks
- Break down 'hire 3 backend engineers' into weekly milestones
